## Full description (4000 char limit)
Gree Local controls compatible air conditioners directly over your own Wi-Fi network.

Air conditioners with a Wi-Fi module speak a simple protocol on the local network. The
manufacturer's apps use it too, but only after routing you through a cloud sign-up. This
app skips that part. It finds units on your network, pairs with them directly, and talks
to them over the wire. No account, no servers, no internet connection required.

WHAT IT DOES
- Finds units automatically by sweeping your local network
- Pairs locally and keeps the key on your phone
- Power, mode (auto, cool, dry, fan, heat), target temperature and fan speed
- Vertical and horizontal louver control, with the positions drawn as you set them
- Turbo, Quiet, Sleep, Display light, X-Fan and Health
- Live room temperature from the unit's own sensor
- A home screen widget for one-tap power and temperature
- Several units, and an add-by-IP fallback for networks that block discovery

PRIVACY
No analytics. No crash reporting. No advertising. No third-party SDKs. The app never
contacts any server. Everything it stores stays on your phone.

WHAT YOU NEED
An air conditioner with a working Wi-Fi module, already joined to the same Wi-Fi network
as your phone. If it has never been set up, join it to your network once first.
